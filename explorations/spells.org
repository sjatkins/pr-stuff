* Two tables
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: two-tables
:END:

** The engine table (=spell_info[]=, =h/spell_func.h=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: engine-table
:END:

One row per spell number: =beats= (casting time in violence pulses),
minimum position, flags (violent, general, area, quest...), a
=targets= bitmask, and the =cast_= function pointer.

#+begin_example
  /* 1 */ {  1, STA, V|G,     CR,              cast_armor },
  /* 2 */ {  1, FIG, V|G|A|Q, CR|VC,           cast_teleport },
  /* 4 */ {  2, FIG, V|G,     CR|VC|VI,        cast_blindness },
#+end_example

Target bits (=TAR_*=): ignore, char in room, char in world, fight
self, fight victim (defaults to your opponent), self only, not self,
object in inventory, in room, in world, equipped, by name, violent
(starts a fight), room. A =cast_X= wrapper switches on how the spell
was invoked, =SPELL_TYPE_SPELL=, potion, wand, staff, scroll or
weapon spell, and calls the real =spell_X(level, caster, arg, type,
target char, target obj, effective level)=.

** The class table (=world/CLASSES/classes=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: class-table
:END:

Each class lists the spells and skills it can learn, one row each:

#+begin_example
SPELLS                 Lvl Diff Cost Gld Max Mana  S  Prereq         Components
magic missile         |  3|  1 |    |   | 95| 14 | P |               |960
continual light       |  7|  1 |    |   | 95| 15 | P | create light  |960
#+end_example

Level to learn, difficulty (multiplies mana when reagents are
missing), cost, guild maximum, maximum learnable percent, mana cost,
source (=P= power or mana), prerequisites, component vnums. Loaded
into =spell_entry= records per class; =do_cast= finds the caster's
class entry for the spell and refuses if there is none.

* Casting (=do_cast=, =spell_parser.c:797=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: casting
:END:

In order, each a refusal with its own message:

1. Not humanoid, in a form that cannot cast, or a paladin or avenger
   off their alignment path.
2. =NO_MAGIC= room (immortals exempt). Underwater needs the right
   spell or form. Bards have a separate =ballad= syntax.
3. Spell name parsed; the class entry found; "unimplemented/disabled"
   if the engine table has no function.
4. Position: the table's minimum, and "can't concentrate" if the spell
   needs more than fighting position and you are fighting.
5. Level, silence, confusion, two druid form checks.
6. Cost: the entry's mana; multiplied by =1 + difficulty= when
   reagents are missing and the caster lacks reagent mastery;
   divided by remort mana or power efficiency. Checked against mana
   or power by =source=; a true-neutral caster without a remort effect
   cannot use mana magic.
7. Delay: =WAIT_QTR_SEC + PULSE_VIOLENCE × (beats - 1)=, reduced by
   casting haste scaled by remort count. Immortals cast instantly.
8. The roll: =d101= against the caster's learned percent for the
   spell; power casters add =power_penalty=, a negative from the
   weight of iron worn and carried ("your focus is warped by the
   presence of iron"). A failed roll costs half the mana, and with a
   second roll under the learned percent the spell /improves/ ("in
   failure, you learn better the nuances").
9. On success: lag =delay + 2 s=, an =EVT_SPELL= scheduled after
   =delay= carrying the spell number and argument, and =say_spell=:
   the room hears the spell's name with each syllable swapped from a
   table ("bless" → "fido", "bur" → "mosa") unless they know the spell.

At completion (=event_spell=, =spell_parser.c:1250=): re-check
silence, confusion, paralysis and position; deduct the cost; resolve
the target /now/ by the target bits (the named character in the room,
your opponent for fight-victim spells, yourself for self-only,
inventory or room objects); refuse if the caster changed rooms; call
the spell function with the caster's level as the effective level.
A target that left during the cast is simply not found.

* What a spell does
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-a-spell-does
:END:

Three shapes cover nearly all 450 functions.

- /Damage/: roll dice scaled by level, apply remort weapon-spell
  bonuses, call =damage(caster, victim, dam, SPELL_X)=
  ([[file:combat.org::#resolution][combat, resolution]]). Magic
  missile is =dice(level/20, 4)=; lightning bolt =min(200, dice(level/20, 8))=.
  =GetSpellType= maps each spell number to a damage type (fire, cold,
  electricity, acid, poison, force, water, light, darkness, magic) so
  resistances and saves apply. Area spells loop the room.
- /Affect/: build an =affected_type= (spell number, duration in
  ticks, =APPLY_= location, modifier, affect bits) and
  =affect_to_char=. Armour is =-10 - remort= to all AC for
  =max(remort + 5, 14)= ticks plus a body-stopping bonus. Protection
  spells set bits (sanctuary, detect invisible, fly).
- /Direct effect/: heal (cure light =dice(2,13) + 4= plus spell power,
  with a critical-heal chance), restore, dispel, teleport and
  relocation, create items, charm, summon, enchant.

=calc_spell_power= adds a fraction of the caster's =spell_power=
(from items, affects and remort) to heals, durations and some damage;
=spell_duration= lengthens affects; =spell_casting_haste= shortens
delays.

The saving throw used by damage and some affects is
=MakeResistanceCheck=: =CalcResistanceCheck= takes the attacker's
level, the victim's resistance value for the damage type and a
"resist stat" blended from the stats that matter for that type (fire
and electricity: dexterity and luck; cold and poison: constitution and
luck; force: strength and constitution), looks it up on a curve, and
the victim passes on =d100 <= min(95, max(scale × score, minimum))=.

* Affects (=handler.c=, =spell_parser.c=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: affects
:END:

An =affected_type= on a character: =type= (spell or skill number),
=duration= in ticks, =modifier=, =location= (an =APPLY_= slot), and
an affect =bitvector=. =affect_to_char= links it at the head of
=ch->affected=, applies the modifier through =affect_modify= (which
handles the special locations: resist and immune bits, susceptibility,
granted spells) and recomputes totals with =affect_total=. Barbarians
cannot be poisoned by this path. =affect_join= handles recasting:
durations and modifiers are averaged or summed per the join flags.

Every tick (=affect_update= → =update_char_spells=) each affect's
duration is reduced; at zero the wear-off message from
=spell_wear_off_msg[]= (=constants.c=) is sent, "You feel less
protected", unless it starts with =*=, and the affect is removed with
its modifier reversed. Remort bonuses and shapeshift forms are exempt
from ageing. The item-set and remort bonus resets walk this same list,
which is where the 2026-09-28 use-after-free was.

* Pulse affects and cooldowns
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: pulse-affects
:END:

A second affect system for things that /happen over time/ rather than
sit as a modifier: =affected_pulse_type= carries a damage type, proc
type with initial, pulse and finish stages, stacking rules
(=can_stack=, multiplier, counter), wall-clock =start_time=,
=last_update=, =pulse_proc_frequency=, =duration=, counters, messages
for start, pulse and finish, and a location and modifier. Every
second (=update_pulse_affects=,
[[file:game-engine-loop.org::#periodic-work-keyed-to-the-pulse-count][engine, periodic work]])
each pulse affect whose frequency has elapsed runs its proc (damage
over time, heal over time, a periodic buff, a shield that absorbs a
percentage), and expires by wall clock, so a server stall ends them
early while ordinary affects freeze. =affected_pulse_cooldown= records
when an ability may be used again (silence, assassinate, item-set
abilities). These are what =PULSE_TYPE_BUFF=, =HEAL=, =DAMAGE=,
=SHIELD= refer to in the headers.

A newer data-driven /Effect/ system exists alongside
(=EffectDefinition= with hashes of messages and procs such as
=RandomDamage=, defined in =world/SKILL/effects.tran=); it is partly
wired and is described in [[file:effects_skills.org][effects_skills.org]].

* Spell items and components
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: items
:END:

- /Potions/ (=quaff=): up to three spells at the item's level, cast
  on the drinker with =SPELL_TYPE_POTION=; the potion is consumed.
- /Scrolls/ (=recite=): the same with a target, consumed.
- /Wands and staves/ (=use=): a level, charges and a spell in the
  value slots; staves hit everyone in the room. Charges count down.
- /Weapon spells/: an =APPLY_WEAPON_SPELL= apply on a weapon fires its
  spell on hits with =SPELL_TYPE_WEAPONSPELL=; item-set weapon spells
  likewise ([[file:combat.org::#resolution][combat]]).
- /Spell gems and pouches/: item types that hold spells or gems for
  socketing ([[file:objects.org::#applies-sockets-sets][objects]]).
- /Components/: a class spell row may list reagent vnums;
  =has_components= requires each in the caster's possession, or a
  casting item, or reagent mastery; otherwise the spell still works
  at the difficulty-multiplied mana cost.

* Learning
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: learning
:END:

A character's known spells and skills are arrays of (number, learned
percent). =practice= at a guildmaster (a mob special,
[[file:mobs.org::#special-procedures][mobs, specials]]) spends
=spells_to_learn= points gained on levelling to raise a percent toward
the class's guild maximum, gated by level and prerequisites; beyond
that, use raises it, including the learn-on-failure roll in casting.
=get_spell= and =get_skill= read the percent; weapon proficiencies are
skills numbered from =PROF_BASE= and scale weapon to-hit and damage
([[file:combat.org::#to-hit][combat, to hit]]). Remort resets every
percent to zero ([[file:players.org::#levels-experience-remort-apex][players, remort]]).

* Mobs and spells
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: mobs
:END:

A mob's spell list from its definition is used two ways: on a strike,
=mob_attack= rolls against =magic_use= and may cast a random known
spell instead of hitting; on a think while fighting, =act_caster= →
=eval_spells= rates each known spell for the situation and casts the
best through =phys_cast=, the same path as a player minus the
command parsing ([[file:mobs.org::#in-a-fight][mobs, in a fight]]).

* Quirks
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: quirks
:END:

- Two affect systems age on different clocks: ordinary affects by
  tick count, pulse affects by wall clock.
- The spell list is three parallel things that must agree by number:
  =h/spells.h= defines, the =spell_info[]= row, and the class file's
  name. A new spell touches all three plus the wear-off message table.
- Failing a cast can improve the spell; succeeding cannot.
- Iron is a power caster's enemy by weight, 150 units worn or 300
  carried per point of penalty.
- A spell's target is resolved when it completes, not when it is
  cast, so a victim who leaves the room mid-cast escapes it.
- Immortal class casts instantly and is exempt from most checks.
- Barbarians ignore the poison affect entirely.
