#+DATE: 2026-10-02

Items: prototypes, instances, where they live, how they load, change,
persist and die. Read from =src/= at commit 94876a77 and the world data
files. Companion to [[file:game-engine-loop.org][game-engine-loop.org]], [[file:mobs.org][mobs.org]], [[file:players.org][players.org]].

See also: [[file:game-engine-loop.org][game-engine-loop.org]] (the per-object tick event), [[file:rooms.org][rooms.org]] (zone resets and zone saves), [[file:players.org][players.org]] (the player file that carries objects), [[file:mobs.org][mobs.org]] (identity, specials dispatch). Earlier extractor notes: [[file:item_sets.org][item_sets.org]], [[file:shops.org][shops.org]], [[file:locker.org][locker.org]], [[file:warehouse.org][warehouse.org]].

* Prototype and instance
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: prototype-and-instance
:END:

A /prototype/ is an entry in =obj_db=, a hash keyed by vnum of
=index_mem= records (=objdb.c:910=), each holding a template =obj_data=,
counts (=world_count= from zone files, =game_count= live,
=num_destroyed=), limits (=max_exist=, default 32000 meaning none;
=max_for_player=; =zone_limit=) and an optional special-procedure
pointer. 4,854 prototypes load from =obj.out= at boot.

An /instance/ is an =obj_data= made by =get_obj(vnum, ...)= as a copy
of the template. It has no identity beyond its memory address and its
vnum: two long swords are two copies of prototype 3022 (see [[file:mobs.org::#no-instance-identity][mobs, "No instance identity"]]). Instance-only state is the location pointers,
timers, contents, sockets, mutations, provenance strings and anything
an immortal changed.

* The struct (=h/structs.h:240=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: the-struct
:END:

| group        | fields                                                                                           |
|--------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| identity     | =vnum=, =name= (keyword list, what =get=/=show= match), =short_description= (as carried: "a long sword"), =description= (as lying in a room), =action_description=, =ex_description= (keyword → text extras) |
| kind         | =type_flag= (one of the item types below), =wear_flags= (where it can go), =extra_flags= (bits below), =material=, =rarity=, =obj_tier=, =min_level= |
| numbers      | =value[5]= and =dvalue[5]= (meaning depends on type), =values[]= for hierarchical items, =cost=, =cost_per_day= (rent), =real_cost=, =qpCost= (quest points) |
| physical     | =intrinsic_weight=, =contents_weight=, =intrinsic_volume=, =contents_volume=, =length=, =width=, =height= |
| time         | =timer=, =timer2=, =time_stamp=, =created_date=, =last_use_time=, =use_cooldown=, =ws_cooldown= |
| modifiers    | =apply[15]= (location, modifier, qualifier), =pulse_apply[15]=, =activate_thresholds[]=, =bitvector[8]= (affects granted while worn) |
| sockets      | =sockets= count, =socket_apply[25]=, =contains_sockets=, =socketed_into= |
| sets         | =item_set=, =piece_of_set=, =item_ability_desc=                                                 |
| provenance   | =loaded_by_imm=, =edited_by_imm=, =mutated_by_imm=, =stringed_by_imm=, =mutation_count=, =owner=, =held_for= |
| lock puzzle  | =mm_crack_code=, =mm_crack_attempts= (crackable containers)                                     |
| location     | =in_room= or =carried_by= or =equipped_by= or =in_obj= or =in_warehouse= / =in_acct_warehouse=; =contains=; list links =next_content=, =next= |

Exactly one of the location pointers is meaningful at a time, and the
code maintains the room, character and container lists by hand
(=obj_to_room=, =obj_from_char=, ...). The whole "where is it" question
is these five pointers plus the intrusive lists; see [[file:mobs.org][mobs.org]] for why
that matters ([[file:mobs.org::#no-instance-identity][mobs, identity]]).

* Item types (=item_types[]=, =constants.c=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: item-types
:END:

undefined, light, scroll, wand, staff, weapon, fire weapon, missile,
treasure, armor, potion, worn, other, trash, trap, container, note,
liquid container, key, food, money, pen, boat, audio, board, book,
pouch, component, hierarchical, spell gem, set item, socket.

The five =value= slots mean different things per type, and the world
file names them per type instead of by index: an armour block has
=effective_ac= and =force=; a weapon its dice and damage type; a light
its hours in =value[2]=; a container its capacity, =CONT_*= flags and
key vnum; a liquid container its capacity, current amount, liquid and
poison; a wand or staff its level, charges and spell; a scroll or
potion its level and up to three spells; a corpse (a container) uses
=value[3]= as the corpse marker and =timer= for decay. The compiler
(=world/compile=, =tran=) maps the names to slots.

** Wear (=wear_flags=, =h/const.h:265=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: wear
:END:

take, finger, neck, body, head, legs, feet, hands, arms, shield, about,
waist, wrist, wield, hold, throw, light, damaged (cannot wear or
wield), construction, pouch, dual wield, dual wield only (off-hand
only), wings, trinket. A character has 22 wear positions (=MAX_WEAR=):
light, two fingers, two neck, body, head, legs, feet, hands, arms,
shield, about, waist, two wrists, wield, hold, off-hand, wings, two
trinkets.

** Extra (=extra_flags=, =h/const.h:292=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: extra
:END:

glow, hum, flexible (volume grows with contents), mundane
(unenchantable), nodrop (cursed in the Diku sense: cannot be dropped or
given), invisible, magic, cursed, bless, anti-good, anti-evil,
anti-neutral, randomize (mutate on load), hidden, indestruct, arena
only, test. Bits 14 to 28 are unused.

** Container (=CONT_*=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: container
:END:

closeable, pickproof, closed, locked, knockable, crackable. A crackable
locked container gets a =mm_crack_code= at load and the =crack= command
is a Mastermind-style guessing game against it.

* Rarity, tiers, mutation
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: rarity-tiers-mutation
:END:

=rarity= is common, uncommon, rare, epic or legendary; it colours the
name everywhere (=rarity_color_code=, yellow through purple) and feeds
the mutation roll. =obj_tier= is a separate scale. =ITEM_RANDOMIZE= on a
prototype, or the immortal =mutateobj= command, calls
=mutate_object(o, min)= (=objdb.c:449=): add =min= plus a critical-chain
roll of extra applies, each with a random "karma" (good or bad), a
location picked to avoid duplicates and a modifier sized from the
per-apply stats table, renaming the item with the apply's adjective or
"of ..." phrase as it goes ("a flaming longsword of the bear"). The
rarity can rise with net karma. Applies are capped at 15 per object.
=mutateinfo= shows the tables.

* Applies, sockets, sets
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: applies-sockets-sets
:END:

- /Applies/: up to 15 (location, modifier, qualifier) triples; 104
  =APPLY_= locations: the seven stats, level, age, mana, hit, move,
  gold, exp, hit and damage rolls, saving throws, resistances,
  immunities and susceptibilities, per-body-part armour and stopping,
  skill bonuses (backstab, kick, sneak, hide, bash, pick, steal,
  track), spells cast on wear, weapon spells, eat spells, power, and
  the elemental resistances. =pulse_apply= entries tick while worn.
  =activate_thresholds= gate applies on level.
- /Sockets/: a socketable item has =sockets= slots; gems (type "spell
  gem" or "socket") inserted with =socket insert= add their applies to
  =socket_apply[]= and are remembered by vnum and name; =socket
  combine= merges gems, =socket destroy= and =remove= undo. =where=
  searches socket names too.
- /Item sets/ ([[file:item_sets.org][item_sets.org]]): =world/ITEM_SETS/item_sets.current= defines named sets
  with two-piece and higher bonuses (applies) and a rarity; a "set
  item" carries =item_set= and its piece number, and wearing enough
  pieces applies the set bonus (=reset_and_apply_item_set_bonuses=,
  =handler.c=, the function whose freed-node loop crashed on 2026-09-28).
  Sets 1 and 2 are reserved placeholders.

* Where objects come from
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: where-objects-come-from
:END:

** Zone resets (=zone.c=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: zone-resets
:END:

The only source of new instances other than immortals and a few
specials ([[file:rooms.org::#ageing-and-reset][rooms, "Ageing and reset"]]). A zone command loads an object into a room (=O=), gives it to
a mob (=G=), equips it (=E=), puts it in a container (=P= / =I=), or
chains loads. =load_obj= (=zone.c:441=) counts how many of that vnum
are already in the room, loads the difference up to the command's
count (a =U= unique command loads at most one), and rolls the command's
load probability per copy; a probability over 100 is treated as about
0.1%. Loads are also refused when the prototype's =max_exist= is
reached, and =max_for_player= caps how many of a limited item one
character may own (checked in =get= and in shops, "You already have N,
the limit"). =show obj limited= lists prototypes with either limit.

** Other sources
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: other-sources
:END:

- =load= (immortal, =I6=) creates an instance and stamps
  =loaded_by_imm=.
- Corpses are containers made by =make_corpse= at death, holding the
  inventory and equipment, with a decay =timer= that is shorter when
  empty.
- Some specials and spells create objects (keys, summoned items).
- Shops with =producing= lists conjure their stock on demand.

* While in the world
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: while-in-the-world
:END:

** Carrying
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: carrying
:END:

Weight and item-count capacity come from strength (=CAN_CARRY_W=,
=CAN_CARRY_N=); containers have their own capacity in =value[0]= and
contribute =contents_weight= and =contents_volume= to whoever holds
them. =ITEM_FLEXIBLE= containers grow with contents. =nodrop= items
cannot be dropped or given; anti-alignment items zap the wrong wearer
on wear.

** The per-object tick (=ev_update_obj=, =limits.c:990=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: the-per-object-tick
:END:

Every object instance has its own =EVT_OBJ_UPDATE= event once per
60-second tick ([[file:game-engine-loop.org::#the-event-system][engine, "The event system"]]), which:

- counts down =timer2= and, at zero, drops a carried item to the floor
  or deletes one inside a container, with "phases out of existence"
  (used for temporary and dropped items, =DROP_TICKS=);
- for armour with a biodegrade value, counts down =timer= and dissolves
  it "from your body", "in your hands" or "into a fertile soil";
- for corpses (containers with the corpse marker), shortens the timer
  once looted empty, counts it down, then spills remaining contents
  into the room and dissolves the corpse;
- handles other timed types (lights burn down their hours while worn in
  the light slot, =handler.c:3552=).

** Sounds
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: sounds
:END:

Objects with noises make them every 60 s on the audio pulse
(=ObjNoise=, =sound.c=), like mobs.

** Specials
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: specials
:END:

13 object special procedures in =spec_obj.c=, bound by vnum in
=assign_objects=. They run with the same trigger types as mob specials
(=SPEC_CMD=, =SPEC_MOVE=, ...) and are checked for every object the
acting player wears or carries, then every object on the floor, after the room special and before the mobs (=interpreter.c:314=; order in [[file:mobs.org::#special-procedures][mobs, "Special procedures"]]).

** Shops (=shop.c=, =world/SHOP=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: shops
:END:

Extracted shop data: [[file:shops.org][shops.org]].

A shop is a keeper mob vnum, a room (0 for a travelling salesman),
lists of types and vnums it buys, what it produces, buy and sell profit
percentages, temper messages and opening hours. Prices scale from
=cost= by the profit percentage and a level scaling; items with a "real
price tag" (=real_cost=) cannot be sold back; limited items respect
=max_for_player= at the counter.

** Auction, lockers, warehouse
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: auction-lockers-warehouse
:END:

Details: [[file:locker.org][locker.org]], [[file:warehouse.org][warehouse.org]].

=auction= is a player market channel. Lockers are per-player object
storage rooms persisted in =LockerSave/*.room= and indexed by
=lockers.save=. The warehouse is an object list on the /account/,
shared by all its characters, persisted in the account's data.

* Persistence
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: persistence
:END:

** In player files (=SaveObjectList=, =player.save.c=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: in-player-files
:END:

A character's inventory and each worn item are written inline into the
character file, recursively for containers, as a =SUB_OBJECT= ...
=END_OF_LIST= tree. =WriteObject= writes the vnum and then only the
fields that differ from the prototype: strings are compared with
=str_cmp= against the template and skipped when equal, so a plain
unmodified sword is little more than its vnum, while a restrung,
mutated or socketed one carries its differences. On load, =ReadObject=
starts from the prototype and overlays the saved fields, which is also
what makes old saves survive prototype changes: anything not saved
takes the current template's value.

** In rooms (=world.save.c=, =SaveZone=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: in-rooms
:END:

Every 10 s one saveable zone (those with a =save= range in the zone
file) has its rooms in that range written to =WorldSave/zone.N= ([[file:rooms.org::#saving][rooms, "Saving"]]),
including objects on the floor, and the world count per prototype. At
boot these are reloaded after the world file, so dropped items in a
saveable zone survive a reboot. =lib/BOOT_CLEAN= discards them.

** Elsewhere
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: elsewhere
:END:

Lockers, the account warehouse, and corpses (which are in rooms, so
only in saveable zones).

* Death of an object
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: death-of-an-object
:END:

=extract_obj= removes it from wherever it is, decrements the
prototype's =game_count= (=handler.c:3990=), frees contents recursively
and frees the struct. Causes: timers above, being eaten, drunk or used
up, sacrifice, junk, =purge= (immortal), a corpse decaying, a container
with it decaying, a player quitting outside a rentable room (equipment
lost), a shop buying it, extraction of its holder, zone resets that
purge rooms, and spells that destroy items. There is no log of any of
this.

* Immortal tools
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: immortal-tools
:END:

=load= / =purge= (=I6=), =string= (=I6=, rename and redescribe,
stamps =stringed_by_imm=), =oedit= (=I10=, add, remove, list, fix
applies; stamps =edited_by_imm=), =mutateobj= (=I9=), =mutateinfo=,
=stat= / =ostat=, =show obj= (search, range, limited), =owhere=,

host ([[file:../SUMMARY.md][SUMMARY.md]] §15).

* Quirks
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: quirks
:END:

- Identity: none per instance. Two copies of a unique item after a
  restore from two backups are indistinguishable; "where did my sword
  go" cannot be answered.
- The in-file representation is a diff against the prototype, so
  editing a prototype silently changes every saved copy that did not
  override that field. Useful for balance changes, surprising for
  restrung items that relied on a default.
- =extra_flags= has fifteen unused bits in the middle and the
  randomize flag sits among them; =ITEM_HIDDEN= exists "for compat
  with 1.5 server".
- Limits: =max_exist= 32000 means unlimited; the only places that
  enforce =max_for_player= are =get= and shops, not zone loads to mobs.
- A load probability over 100 is a deliberate near-zero rate, not a
  bug.
- Light hours, corpse decay, biodegrade and drop timers are all
  separate counters on the same two fields, interpreted by type.
