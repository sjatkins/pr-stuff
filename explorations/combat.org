#+TITLE: Perilous Realms: combat
#+DATE: 2026-10-02

How fights start, how a blow is resolved, what reduces it, what happens
at zero, and who gets the experience. Read from =src/fight.c= (5,494
lines), =offensive.c=, =limits.c= at commit 94876a77.

See also: [[file:game-engine-loop.org::#the-event-system][engine, the event system]]
(strikes are events), [[file:mobs.org::#in-a-fight][mobs, in a fight]]
(what mobs do between blows), [[file:players.org::#death][players, death]],
[[file:objects.org::#applies-sockets-sets][objects, applies]] (where
hit and damage bonuses come from), [[file:rooms.org::#room-flags][rooms, flags]]
(peaceful and arena rooms), [[file:spells.org][spells.org]] (casting, affects, saves).

* Shape of a fight
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: shape
:END:

There is no round. Each combatant has its own chain of =EVT_STRIKE=
events on the wheel; each blow schedules the next one after that
attacker's own speed (=calc_speed=, below). Two fighters with
different speeds simply interleave. A fight is the pair of =fighting=
pointers plus whatever strike chains exist; it ends when one side
stops (=stop_fighting=), leaves the room, flees, or dies.

Starting: =hit=, =kill=, a skill, a spell, mob aggression or a hunt
arriving all end up in =set_fighting(ch, victim)= (=fight.c=), which
refuses self, dead victims and mobs flagged =ACT_IMMORTAL=, caps a
mob's simultaneous attackers at six, records =fighting= both ways, and
schedules the first strike after =calc_speed(ch)= pulses. If the
attacker is the aggressor against a player, =make_criminal= is called
([[#pk][PK]]).

Per strike (=event_strike=): bail if the fight is over or the victim
left the room; a mob develops hatred for its target
([[file:mobs.org::#memory-hatred-and-fear][mobs, memory]]); a mob tries
=mob_attack= first, a roll against its =magic_use= chance that picks a
random known skill or spell to use instead of a plain blow; otherwise
=hit=; then schedule the next strike.

Limits: =ok_to_fight= refuses fights in =PEACEFUL= rooms, across rooms,
and when a victim already has 10 attackers or the attacker 12.

* Speed (=calc_speed=, =calc_speed_percent=, =limits.c=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: speed
:END:

One blow per second is par (100%). Delay in pulses is =100 ×
PULSE_PER_SEC / percent=.

- players: =100 + 10 × level / class speed_level= (default 500), times
  the weapon's speed value (=value[4]=, 10 = bare hands) over 10,
  times a dexterity factor, capped at =10 × class speed_max=;
- mobs: =attacks_per_round × 100=, times a half-strength dexterity
  factor;
- then =melee_haste= from items and affects.

So a level 500 player with a speed-15 weapon and good dexterity strikes
about twice a second; a mob with =attacks_per_round { 3 }= strikes
three times a second.

* Dual wield
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: dual-wield
:END:

After the main-hand blow, if both hands hold weapons the held one can
wear =ITEM_WIELD= (and =ITEM_DUAL_WIELD= unless the attacker is a
thief), a roll under the dual-wield skill lets the off-hand strike in
the same event, at a 1-in-(speed difference) chance when the off-hand
weapon is slower. Off-hand to-hit is 10 worse; off-hand damage gets
the remort dual-wield multiplier.

* To hit (=hit=, =calc_thac0=, =effective_ac=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: to-hit
:END:

1. =set_hitaim_loc=: the attacker's =aimloc= (default body) is hit if
   =d20 < dexterity - victim intelligence/2=; otherwise a random
   location from a table indexed by the height-class difference
   (tall attackers hit heads, short ones feet). Skills always hit the
   body.
2. Attacker's THAC0: for players =THACO(ch) - 7 × hitroll=, where
   =THACO= is =max(200 - 10 × level / class thaco_level, class
   thaco_min × 10)=; for mobs =200 - 10 × hitroll= (or a flat -40 for
   mobs above level 500 with low hitroll). Plus half of the missing
   weapon proficiency (=100 - skill= over 2), minus the strength to-hit
   bonus, plus 10 for the off-hand. Floor -75.
3. Victim's AC at that location: =armor[loc]= plus the dexterity
   defensive bonus if standing, clamped to ±100.
4. Roll =d200=. Hit if the roll is over 196, or the victim is not
   awake, or =thac0 - ac <= roll=. A roll under 5 always misses.

A miss still calls =damage= with 0 so messages and bookkeeping run; a
missed backstab or assassination has its own consequences.

* Damage of a blow (=hit_landed=, =base_attack_damage=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: blow-damage
:END:

Base: the strength damage bonus, plus the weapon's dice
(=value[1]= d =value[2]=) scaled by the attacker's proficiency in that
weapon type (=value[0]=) for players, or the mob's own damage dice
plus =extradam=; bare hands add 0 to 2. Then =dam_bonus=, remort
bonuses and criticals: a critical threat built from remort count and
precision decides, on a d200, between normal, improved and "sprays
blood" criticals, with ninjas and the crit remort effect widening the
window.

Multipliers: victims below fighting position take more; location
multiplies head 1.11, body 1.07, arms 1.04, legs 1.02, feet 1.0;
off-hand blows get the dual-wield remort bonus.

* Resolution (=damage=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: resolution
:END:

Every source of harm, blows, skills, spells, room damage, poison,
suffering, goes through =damage(ch, victim, dam, type)=. In order:

1. =ok_to_fight=; start the fight both ways if it is not already on.
2. Spell damage may be absorbed by a globe shield, saved against
   (=MakeResistanceCheck=), or modified by spell power.
3. =CheckResistanceAttackType=: map the attack to a damage type (fire,
   cold, water, electricity, poison, acid, force, magic, light,
   darkness, plus the physical kinds) and apply immunity (zero),
   resistance and susceptibility from the victim's =resistances= and
   =M_resist= / =M_immune= / =susc= bits, with a saving roll where the
   type allows one. For melee, =PreProcDam=.
4. Stopping: =stopping[hitloc] + worn stopping bonus - attacker's
   melee penetration=, scaled, is subtracted; sanctuary adds a flat
   stopping bonus (with a class and wisdom modifier) rather than
   halving; susceptibility reduces stopping.
5. Pulse-affect absorbs (a percentage shield) take their cut; damage
   dealt may heal the attacker through life steal or weapon vamp.
6. =DamageStuff=: unless in the arena, heavy blows may damage a random
   worn item (=DamageOneItem=, never pouches, indestructibles, or vnum
   25; remort durability resists).
7. =hit_shield= absorbs before hit points; then =hit -= dam=.
8. =update_pos=: hit points above 0 keep or restore the position
   (stunned if paralysed); 0 to -2 stunned, -3 to -5 incapacitated,
   -6 to -10 mortally wounded, -11 and below dead.
9. Messages from the =messages= file by damage keyword and band, to
   attacker, victim and room; the victim's condition message when
   below sleeping.
10. Wimpy: a player whose =whimpy_level= is set flees at that
    threshold; =ACT_WIMPY= mobs flee when hurt.
11. If the victim died: =die=.

Position matters afterwards: incapacitated and mortally wounded
characters lose hit points every tick until they die or are healed
([[file:game-engine-loop.org::#the-event-system][the per-character tick]]).

* Death (=die=, =raw_kill=, =make_corpse=, =group_gain=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: death
:END:

=die=: the mob special gets =SPEC_DEATH=; a player's death actions and
experience penalty ([[file:players.org::#death][players, death]]);
alignment shifts for the killer (=change_alignment=); =death_cry= to
neighbouring rooms; experience to the killer's group; then
=raw_kill=: =make_corpse= (a container with everything the dead one
carried and wore, a decay timer shorter when empty,
[[file:objects.org::#the-per-object-tick][objects, per-object tick]]),
mobs are extracted, players are moved to =LOAD_ROOM_MORTAL= standing
with 1 hit point. Prototype =num_destroyed= increments.

Experience (=group_gain=): the victim's experience value is divided by
the sum of levels of the group members present in the room (or just
the killer), multiplied by a gain multiplier and the clan multiplier,
then given to each member scaled by their level through the per-class
=EXP_NEEDED= curve. No experience for killing players.

* Fleeing (=do_flee=, =offensive.c=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: fleeing
:END:

Refused while falling, paralysed, badly lagged, held by clinging
vines, berserking, or below stunned. A sitting or resting character
first scrambles up with two seconds of lag. Then a roll: a bad one
means "you blindly run into something" and lag; otherwise a random
exit is chosen among the room's open ones, "panics, and attempts to
flee", and the move is attempted, failing if too exhausted. Outside the
arena a successful flee costs experience, and a mob left behind
starts hunting ([[file:mobs.org::#hunting-chasing-across-rooms][mobs, hunting]]).

* Skills (=offensive.c=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: skills
:END:

Thirty-odd commands: hit, kill, backstab, bash, kick, batter, pummel,
rescue, assist, vital stab, execute, berzerk, sunder armour,
preparation, slice'n'dice, vanish, daze, ambush, cheapshot, garrote,
mute strike, whirlwind, crippling strike, strike, holy strike, breath,
shadowform, assassinate, and =wimp= to set the flee threshold. The
common shape: find the target, refuse in peaceful rooms, refuse while
berserking, refuse self, check position and height class, roll against
the skill percentage, apply lag with =WAIT_STATE=, and call =damage=
with the skill as the type (so hit location is the body and messages
are the skill's). Backstab needs a wielded weapon and at most two
other attackers. Each skill's number, position requirement and
minimum level come from the skill table (=world/SKILL=,
[[file:players.org::#classes-races-clans][effects and skills]]).

* Spells in combat
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: spells
:END:

A cast is a command that lags the caster and schedules =EVT_SPELL=
for the completion ([[file:game-engine-loop.org::#the-event-system][engine]]).
Damage spells arrive at =damage= with the spell as the type; the
victim's saving roll is =MakeResistanceCheck(attacker, victim,
modifier, damage type, minimum, scale)=, a d100 against a resistance
score derived from level, stats and the victim's resistances, clamped
to 95. Mobs cast through =mob_attack= on their strikes and through
=act_caster= on their thinks.

* PK
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: pk
:END:

PK is global: the old world-based restriction is commented out in
=ok_to_fight= ("MAKING IT GLOBAL PK"); only =PEACEFUL= rooms and the
arena rules apply. Attacking a player you are not already fighting
makes you a criminal (=crime.c=, =find_criminal= / =make_criminal=):
police mobs and charmed followers are exempt, the arena is exempt, and
the aggressor gets a =rent_disable= of 15 minutes (extended on
repeat), during which they cannot quit or camp
([[file:players.org::#leaving][players, leaving]]) and link loss adds
more. Immortals do not count. Arena rooms suspend item damage and
experience loss and have their own contest handling.

* Quirks
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: quirks
:END:

- A fight is two pointers; if either side's pointer is cleared without
  the other's, one combatant keeps swinging at nothing until the next
  strike notices. =SwitchTargets= and =FindAnAttacker= exist to patch
  this up.
- The six-attacker cap is enforced in =set_fighting=, the ten and
  twelve in =ok_to_fight=; they disagree.
- "Bonecruncher won't let you" is a hardcoded weapon check in flee.
- Object vnum 25 can never be damaged, by number.
- =PULSE_VIOLENCE= survives in name only.
- Level 500+ mobs with hitroll under 20 get a flat THAC0 of -40, a
  patch for a tuning problem rather than a rule.
